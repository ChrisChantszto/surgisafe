SurgiSafe-RT

Real-time safety guardian for a remote, AI-assisted ultrasound robot · educational simulation, not for clinical use

Connecting…

The scenario. A sonographer in another city scans a patient through a robot arm, steering the ultrasound probe with hand gestures (camera AI) or letting an AI auto-scan. The probe must press on the skin gently (5 N at most) and must never enter the fresh surgical wound. The AI and the network are not trusted: every move is checked by a QNX safety monitor, which stops the arm if a rule is broken or the commands go silent.
